⚠ MODEL CAVEATS — read before citing any number

D1 · Seat-share proxy: Dirichlet means use 2024 seat shares (~52.9% NDA), not real vote shares (~36–46%). P(majority) numbers are upward-biased in level; directional findings are robust.
D2 · Variance too tight: Concentration=18 gives ±11.5pp per-seat std. Real TCPD constituency data shows ±21pp (correct ≈ 5). Winner's bonus is ~1.8× understated in absolute level.
D3 · Not a forecast: Anchored to 2024. Political realignment, new alliances, and delimitation outcomes are not modelled.
Coin-flip principle: More seats do not make elections fairer. They narrow the spread around whatever mean already exists. If that mean is above 50%, more seats entrench the existing lead further — not reduce it.
