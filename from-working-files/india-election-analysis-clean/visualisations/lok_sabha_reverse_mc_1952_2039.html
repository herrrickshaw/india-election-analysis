⚠ Model caveats — read before citing any number

Historical seat counts: From ECI declared results and Wikipedia. Pre-1999 Congress and BJP totals include their pre-coalition predecessors and alliance partners. Reversal is a pure thought experiment — the actual political conditions (Gandhi dynasty incumbency, RSS organisational network, regional parties) that produced these results are not replicated by swapping numbers.
Vote shares: National totals from ECI press notes. Pre-liberalisation elections (1952–1989) used a very different party system; treating "BJP" in 1952 as "Jana Sangh" conflates distinct organisations.
Monte Carlo bias: Uses 2024 state-level geographic distribution (which states have which seats). Pre-delimitation elections (all pre-2026) had different constituency maps. The simulation engine inherits D1 (seat-share proxy, upward bias ~7–17pp on P(majority)) and D2 (concentration=18, variance understated ~1.8×).
Future projections: Linear trend extrapolation on 1999–2024 data. Indian politics has never followed a linear trend for more than 2–3 elections. These are illustrative distributions under stated assumptions, not forecasts.
