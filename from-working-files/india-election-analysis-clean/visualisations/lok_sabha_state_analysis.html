Scenario outcomes · 3,000 Monte Carlo simulations per configuration · Dirichlet concentration = 5
What these scenarios show: Starting from 2024's actual state-level bloc shares (or 2029 trend projections), each scenario reallocates seats proportionally to 2024 electoral roll size, then simulates 3,000 elections. Because 2024 was a strong NDA majority, P(NDA) is high in all scenarios anchored to 2024 shares — the analytically interesting dimension is how many NDA seats the reallocation produces and which states drive those gains, not the win probability alone.

D1 · Seat-share proxy: NDA vote shares derived from 2024 seat shares (~47% national mean), not real ECI vote shares (~37%). P(majority) numbers are upward-biased. Directional findings on reallocation effects are robust.
D2 · Concentration parameter: Dirichlet conc=5 (empirically calibrated to real TCPD constituency-level variance ±21pp). Previous analyses used conc=18 which understated variance 1.8×.
D3 · 2029 projections: Linear trend on 1999–2024. Indian politics has never followed a linear trend for more than 2–3 elections. Treat as illustrative direction, not forecast.
D4 · Historical data 1999–2019: TCPD Lok Dhaba GA file; 2024 from ECI declared results. Pre-1999 state-level breakdown not available in TCPD GA export.
